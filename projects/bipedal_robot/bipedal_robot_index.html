Bipedal Self Stabilizing Robot

Independent robotics project exploring a wheeled biped-style inverted pendulum robot with bending legs, self-stabilizing PID control, compliant joint mechanisms, and practical mechanical build composition.

Project Goal

This active development project is focused on building a compact wheeled biped-style robot that can balance, steer, and eventually use leg motion as part of banked turning and jumping. The goal is to practice full-system robotics design: mechanism layout, actuator selection, sensor feedback control, PID and Kalman filter tuning, electronics, and iterative prototyping.

Current Design Work

Body ideas and overall robot form concepts

Compliant damped servo joint concept for leg impact absorption

Leg mechanism concept combining servo controlled pulley linkage, and BLDC wheel motor

Development Focus

The project is currently in the mechanical concept and electrical system development phase. Currently building and testing electrical subsystems including servo and BLDC motor actuation, and light and sound capability. As the electronics get built out, the CAD for the first iteration of the robot is being made to house it.

Next Steps

The next stage is to develop the leg mechanism and its CAD, including selection of mechanical components such as fasteners and pulleys/cables. As the design matures, this page will be updated with CAD progress, build photos, control testing, and lessons learned from each iteration.
